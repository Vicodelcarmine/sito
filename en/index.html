The place

In Naples an alley
is called a vico

Vico del Carmine is a piece of Naples in the Oltrarno: a trattoria and pizzeria where you eat like at home, with Neapolitan pizzas, seafood pasta, grilled meat and traditional desserts. Simple, generous food, made for sharing.

  • Neapolitan pizza

    The classics and our own Vico pizzas, also with special doughs.

  • Sea and land

    Seafood chitarrina, gnocchi alla sorrentina, sliced steak and grills.

  • The menu in 9 languages

    Flip through it on your phone like a book. At the table you can even order.
